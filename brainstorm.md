Step 2.1 — Brainstorming Ideas

Car Maintenance Tracker
Users log maintenance tasks (oil change, tire rotation, etc.) and get reminders.
Firebase stores all data.

Car Specs Explorer
Browse car models, view specs, and compare cars.

Fuel (MPG) Logger
Track fuel fill-ups, calculate MPG, and show stats.

Step 2.2 - Evaluations
Idea A — Car Maintenance Tracker

The Car Maintenance Tracker is a strong mobile-focused idea with a clear story and high habit potential, fits well in the market, and has a perfectly manageable scope for a semester project.

Idea B — Car Specs Explorer

The Car Specs Explorer has an easy scope and works well on mobile, but it lacks a strong story or habit formation and faces a competitive market.

Idea C — Fuel (MPG) Logger

The MPG Logger offers a practical mobile use case with a clear story and moderate habit potential, though its scope is slightly more complex and the market already has similar apps.

Step 2.3 — The Final Decision

After reviewing all three ideas using the evaluation criteria—Mobile, Story, Market, Habit, and Scope—we decided that the Car Maintenance Tracker app is the strongest and most practical choice for our project. It clearly supports mobile functionality since users often track maintenance while near their vehicle, and it has a meaningful story that helps users prevent costly repairs by staying organized. This idea also encourages habit-building through regular logging and reminders and fits perfectly within our development scope using Firebase for data storage.
